Prepare for your consultation

Organize your questions and the information you would like to discuss with the physician.

1

The reason for your visit.

Describe your concerns and the information you are looking for. You do not need to choose a treatment before requesting a consultation.

2

Your medical history.

Prepare a list of medications, supplements, allergies, and current treatments to discuss with the physician. Ask the team which documents to bring.

3

Your questions.

Write down what you want to know about the options, risks, expectations, and cost.

Before making a decision

Understand the proposal.

Ask what is being recommended, why, and what alternatives are available.

Clarify expectations.

Discuss the available evidence, limitations, and how progress would be assessed.

Review the next steps.

Ask about costs, aftercare, follow-up, and whom to contact with questions.

Traveling to Tijuana from another city?

Confirm availability, location, and arrangements for your visit before planning your trip. If you prefer care in English, ask the team about availability.

Check availability

Take the first step: request a consultation

Contact us to check availability, ask about consultation fees, and get answers before your visit.
